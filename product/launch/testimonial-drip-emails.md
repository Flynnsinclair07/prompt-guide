# Testimonial collection — 3-email drip (Day 7, 14, 30)

A 3-email drip to send buyers of The Job Hunter's AI Bundle. Each email is short, low-pressure, and asks for something specific. The goal: a couple of strong, permission-granted quotes you can put on the Gumroad listing, the homepage, and the PH listing as updates.

**Send from:** flynnsinclair07@icloud.com (your support email; replies forward to iCloud).
**Identify buyers:** Gumroad gives you the buyer's email on each sale. Tag them in Kit (or just a manual spreadsheet for the first 20 buyers) and trigger the drip on purchase date.
**Don't pay for testimonials.** No discounts, no bonuses for replying. Voluntary quotes are the only ones that matter.

## Email 1 — Day 7 (the soft ask)

**Subject:** quick question about the bundle

---

hey [first name],

it's been about a week since you picked up the Job Hunter's AI Bundle — thanks again. I build this stuff solo, so it genuinely means a lot.

if you've had a chance to use any of the prompts, I'd love to hear one or two honest sentences about how it went. no need to be polished:

- what were you stuck on before you bought it?
- which prompt did you actually use?
- did anything come of it (a callback, a sharper resume, a less-rough cover letter)?

even "it's fine, here's what I'd change" is useful. I read every reply.

— flynn
snipprompts.com

p.s. no upsell, no automation here. just a real email asking how it went.

## Where the quotes go (in priority order)

Once you have permission, paste the quote in these places:

1. **Gumroad listing description** — top, above the fold. The buyer-flow is the highest-leverage placement.
2. **Homepage** — short testimonial strip near the bundle banner. Two quotes; rotate quarterly.
3. **PH listing** — post a top-level "update" comment 5–7 days post-launch with the quote and a thank-you. PH listings keep getting traffic for two weeks.
4. **Newsletter** — drop one quote at the bottom of Issue 2 or 3 (next to your sign-off) as light social proof.

## Rules

- **Never quote without permission in writing.** Email reply counts; verbal does not.
- **Never edit meaning.** Light trims (cutting filler, fixing typos) are OK. Rewording is not.
- **Honor anonymity instantly.** "Anonymous, [role]" is real social proof and uses no one against their wishes.
- **Keep a running list.** Save permitted quotes in `product/launch/testimonials-collected.md` (create on first quote) so you can find them when you need them.
- **No fake quotes.** Don't write them yourself; don't ask AI for them; don't paraphrase a comment from elsewhere. One real quote beats five made-up ones.

## What NOT to send

- No "leave a review on Trustpilot / G2 / Capterra" — your product isn't there, and asking burns goodwill.
- No "share this on social" CTAs. Asking buyers to do marketing for you is a fast way to lose them.
- No "I'd love to hop on a quick call" — you don't want to extract testimonials; you want them to want to share.
- No automated "we noticed you bought" — every email above is from a real person with a real first name. Manual is the brand.
