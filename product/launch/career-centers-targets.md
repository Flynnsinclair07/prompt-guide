# Career Center Outreach — First Batch of 20

**Summary:** Of the 20 targets, 13 have verified direct director emails confirmed on a school page; 3 are strong inferred (pattern-matched format with high confidence — flagged); 4 need verification or have only the general dept inbox. Best hooks (top 3): Bucknell (just launched Hiration as their AI resume tool — directly aligned with SnipPrompts), Marquette (Career Closet relaunch in Iggy's Market Jan 2025 — concrete first-line angle), Lewis & Clark (brand-new $5M Career Accelerator with 30 paid internships — major recent news). Patterns: Midwest + small private liberal arts schools were easiest — directors are usually one or two people and bios are public. Mountain West / public regionals (Boise State, Montana State) were harder because the "director" line is often masked behind a general inbox; the Pacific Northwest and Northeast Catholic schools (Providence, Villanova, Marquette, Creighton, Gonzaga) had the cleanest public staff pages.
