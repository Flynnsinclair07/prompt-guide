## What you're doing

Registering with **Disability Services at CU Boulder** so you can get formal accommodations (extended test time, alternate format materials, note-taking support, etc.) that fit your reading-comprehension needs.

This is **standard practice** for incoming students with documented learning differences. Not stigmatized, not extra paperwork to dread — universities expect this and have a streamlined process.

---

## The steps

1. **Visit the Disability Services site:** https://www.colorado.edu/disabilityservices/

2. **Read their "Get Started" page** for new students:
   - URL: https://www.colorado.edu/disabilityservices/students/get-started
   - It will walk through the documentation they require.

3. **Gather your documentation.** They typically accept:
   - High school IEP or 504 plan paperwork (if you had one)
   - Psychoeducational evaluation report from a qualified evaluator (if you have one)
   - A letter from a doctor, psychologist, or educational specialist describing your reading-comprehension diagnosis
   - If you don't have any of these, contact DS and ask what they need from you to start the process — they don't gatekeep, they help

4. **Submit the online intake form** on the Disability Services site:
   - Form URL (verify in case it changes): linked from "Get Started" page above
   - Upload your documentation

5. **Schedule your intake meeting** with a DS advisor:
   - This is usually a 30-60 min conversation about what accommodations will help
   - Can be in-person or virtual
   - Bring a list of any accommodations you used in high school + any new ones you think might help

6. **Confirm your accommodations letter** is generated and you understand how to share it with professors. (Usually you forward it through DS's portal, not directly to professors.)

7. **Done.** Your accommodations apply automatically each semester — you don't have to re-register.
