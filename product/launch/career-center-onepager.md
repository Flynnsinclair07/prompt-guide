# Career Center One-Pager — for "tell me more" replies

**When to use:** a director replies to your cold email asking for more info, a one-pager, or "how would this work for our students." Paste the version below into your reply (or send as a short PDF/Google Doc if they ask for an attachment). It's written to make linking the resource an easy yes.

**Two formats below:** (1) a short email reply, (2) a one-page document version if they want something to forward internally.

---

```
hey [Name],

What it is: snipprompts.com is a free library of 140+ ChatGPT prompts covering the whole job search — resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn, interview prep, networking, and salary negotiation. No signup, no paywall, no email capture. Students just open a page, copy the prompt, and use it in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

- a refuse-to-invent gate — the prompt makes the model ask for real numbers instead of fabricating metrics (the #1 thing that gets students caught in interviews)
- a banned-phrase list — kills the "results-driven, passionate about" clichés that mark a document as AI-written
- post-output rules — what to check after the draft, so students don't paste and submit blindly

Happy to answer anything else, hop on a quick call, or send a list of the pages most relevant to your students. Whatever's easiest for you.
```
