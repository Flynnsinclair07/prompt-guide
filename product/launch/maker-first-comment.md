# PH Maker's first comment — draft + review checklist

This is the FIRST comment you post on your own PH listing, right after it goes live. It's the most-read comment on any PH product (PH ranks it under "Comments" and many viewers read it before the description). Its job: set the tone, surface the failure-mode hook, and invite specific replies you can engage.

**Goal:** specific story + specific question. Not "thanks for visiting!"

---


## Pre-launch review checklist (your hands, ~5 min)

Pull up your scheduled PH listing in a private/incognito window. Read the listing as a stranger. Score yourself on each:

- [ ] **First 4 words of the tagline** — does it say what this is? (Don't open with "The most" / "An AI tool" / vague openers.)
- [ ] **Description first paragraph** — does it open with the problem (AI sounds like AI) or with the product? Problem-first wins on PH.
- [ ] **Gallery images** — first image is strong enough to stand alone if a viewer only sees one. No stale "Launches May 17" text. No old pricing.
- [ ] **Topics** — Artificial Intelligence + Productivity + Career picked. Three is the sweet spot; one is undertagged, five looks spammy.
- [ ] **Main link** — `https://snipprompts.com` (homepage), not Gumroad.
- [ ] **Maker's first comment** — drafted and ready to paste at minute zero (use one of the two versions above).
- [ ] **Pinned reply** — if PH lets you pin a reply to your own maker comment, prepare one: "if you DM me your worst AI output, i'll write a prompt that catches that failure mode and send it back." That's a high-conversion ask.
- [ ] **Scheduled time** — confirmed Wed May 27, 12:01 AM PT (= 1:01 AM MDT).

## What to do RIGHT AFTER launch (Wed 1:01 AM MDT — auto-fires, you're asleep)

You'll wake up to data. First check: is your maker comment live? If yes (which it should be — set it up to post right at launch in PH's scheduler), engage the early replies before work.

If PH didn't auto-post your maker comment for any reason, paste it the moment you see the listing is live. The first comment slot is the most-read piece of real estate on your launch page.

## After-the-fact: what if the launch is quiet?

- A quiet PH launch with 5-20 upvotes is *normal* for a product with no pre-existing audience. Don't panic-edit the listing.
- The maker comment's question ("what's the worst AI output you've ever gotten?") performs best when seeded by 1-2 friends willing to answer in the first 2 hours. Ask 2-3 people from IH or your inner circle to drop a real, useful answer at minute 30 if traffic is low.
- Wednesday's PH numbers do NOT determine launch success. The gate is Thursday. See `ph-gate-read-thursday.md`.
