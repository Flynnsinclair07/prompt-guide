## 1. University of Vermont — Sarah Heath

**To:** Sarah.Heath@uvm.edu

```
hey Sarah,

saw that the Career Interest Group model UVM published with NACE last year is built around cohort-based career conversations rather than 1:1 counseling — that scale-without-losing-quality angle is exactly the gap a free prompt library can help fill outside group time.

quick intro: I'm a CU Boulder freshman starting in the fall, and I've spent the last 8 months building snipprompts.com — a free library of 182 ChatGPT prompts covering resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, interview prep, and salary negotiation. No signup, no paywall, no email capture required to use any of them.

students are using ChatGPT for job-search work whether career centers endorse it or not. the risk is they're using one-line prompts that produce generic output, or worse, prompts that quietly invent experience they don't have. the free pages on snipprompts.com are structured to prevent that — every prompt has guardrails that tell the model to ask for missing info rather than fabricate it, plus a banned-phrase list to keep the output from sounding like AI.

if it'd be useful for your students, you're welcome to link to it from your resources page or newsletter. happy to answer any questions about how the prompts are built.

— Flynn
snipprompts.com
```
