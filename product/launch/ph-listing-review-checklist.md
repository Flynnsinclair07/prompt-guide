## Scheduling
- [ ] Launch is scheduled for **Wed May 27, 12:01 AM PT** (= 1:01 AM MDT). Confirm the date AND timezone in the scheduler.
- [ ] Status shows "Scheduled," not "Draft." (A draft won't auto-publish.)

## Core listing
- [ ] **Name:** SnipPrompts (or "The Job Hunter's AI Bundle" — match whichever you decided to feature).
- [ ] **Tagline:** under 60 chars, no period, says what it is. No banned hype words.
- [ ] **Description:** leads with the problem (AI sounds like AI / invents experience), not the feature list.
- [ ] **Topics/categories:** correct ones selected (Artificial Intelligence, Productivity, Career, and/or Marketing). Max relevance, not max count.
- [ ] **Links:** the main link points to the right place (homepage `snipprompts.com` for the free library, or the Gumroad bundle — match your strategy). Click it and confirm it loads.

## Media
- [ ] **Thumbnail/logo:** crisp, not pixelated, readable at small size.
- [ ] **Gallery images:** in the right order, first one is the strongest. No stale text ("Launches May 17," old pricing, "119-page").
- [ ] **Demo video:** if it's ready, it's attached and plays. If not, the gallery still stands on its own (don't block launch on the video).
- [ ] First gallery image works as a standalone "what is this" if someone only looks at one.

## First comment (maker comment)
