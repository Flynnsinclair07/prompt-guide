**Goal:** monetize the "Go Deeper" sections on ~10 prompt pages with affiliate links to courses. The Amazon book links are already wired in (`tag=promptguide-20`); this layer is for the online-course slot.

## Recommendation

**Apply now: Skillshare.**

**Apply after Product Hunt launch (assuming it lifts traffic past 1k uniques/mo): Coursera.**

The two-program setup gives you (a) immediate income while you're sub-1k uniques and (b) the higher-leverage program once you clear the gate. Don't run all five — fewer programs = cleaner attribution and less link rot to maintain across 10 prompt pages.

---

## 1. Skillshare — apply now

**Why it fits:**
- No traffic minimum stated. Approval in 1–2 business days. Accepts content sites with a clear topical fit, which you have (career, business, productivity prompts).
- 30-day cookie is competitive.
- The commission per new paid member (~$34 cap reported; some sources say 40% flat) is small per conversion but covers more of your prompt pages than Coursera does — Skillshare has strong inventory in productivity, writing, design, freelancing, the categories your business and lifestyle prompts already overlap.
- Single network (Impact) to share with Coursera later — one publisher account, two programs.

**Watch for:**
- Commission per conversion is modest. If you only get 1–2 conversions/month from this in year one, that's a $30–70/month income line, not a meaningful one.
- The "20% vs 40%" inconsistency across affiliate-review sites suggests the program has changed structure at least once. Read the contract terms in Impact carefully before you wire links.

**Best-fit prompt pages for Skillshare links:**
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-content-ideas` → Skillshare content/social courses
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-copywriting` → Skillshare copywriting courses
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-freelance-proposal` → Skillshare freelancing courses
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-creative-writing` → Skillshare writing courses
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-instagram-bio` / `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-instagram-story-ideas` → Skillshare social media courses

**Signup link (direct):** https://www.skillshare.com/en/affiliates

That page hands you off to Impact to register a publisher account.

## 2. Coursera — apply after the Product Hunt launch

**Why it fits (eventually):**
- 20% baseline / **up to 45%** on Specializations, Professional Certificates, Coursera Plus monthly subs. The Specializations and Certificates are the high-ticket items ($49–$79/mo, multi-month commitments) — one conversion can be a $20–50 commission.
- 30-day cookie.
- Coursera inventory is dense in the career-skills and job-hunt-adjacent categories that your highest-traffic pages will be in: data analysis, project management, Excel, programming, communication, leadership, etc.

**Why not now:**
- Hard requirement: 1,000 unique monthly visitors. Pre-PH, you don't have it. Applying before you clear the gate risks a rejection that may take 30+ days to retry cleanly.
- Manual review process means an early rejection sits in your file. Better to apply once when you have the analytics screenshot to send with the application.

**Trigger to apply:** when Google Analytics / Cloudflare Analytics shows >1,000 unique visitors in a single 30-day window. Realistic timeline: 2–4 weeks post-PH if the launch lands.

**Best-fit prompt pages for Coursera links:**
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-data-analysis` → Coursera data analytics certificates
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-excel-formulas` → Coursera Excel courses
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-investing-beginners` → Coursera finance courses
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-learning-a-language` → Coursera language courses
- `best-chatgpt-prompt-for-coding` → Coursera CS certificates (Google IT, Meta Front-End, etc. — these are the highest-converting items in the catalog)

**Signup link (direct):** https://www.coursera.org/about/affiliates

That page links to the Coursera program inside Impact. Same publisher account as Skillshare.

---

## 3. Udemy — skip

**Why not:**
- 7-day cookie is 75% shorter than the 30-day standard.
- 15% commission combined with Udemy's near-permanent sale prices ($10–$15) means typical commissions are $1–$2 per sale.
- Volume model: you'd need 30–50 conversions/month to make it worth the link maintenance, and that's not realistic on current traffic.

**When it could change:** if you ever build a "ChatGPT for [skill]" series (e.g. ChatGPT for Excel, ChatGPT for Python) where the implied audience is hobbyist-priced course-buyers, Udemy starts to make sense. Not today.

---

## 4. LinkedIn Learning — skip

**Why not:**
- Acceptance rate <5%. Even with relevant content and clean site, rejection is the base case.
- The commission ($10 free trial / 35% purchase) is fine, but the application cost (time, risk of denial) is too high pre-launch.

**When to revisit:** post-Product Hunt, after you have traffic numbers and 30+ days of clean Impact history from Skillshare. LinkedIn Learning's gatekeeping is a function of your existing affiliate trust — easier to get in as a second program than as a first.

---

## 5. MasterClass — skip for this site

**Why not:**
- MasterClass courses are aspirational (celebrity-taught, story-driven) and don't map cleanly to the practical, do-the-task prompt pages on snipprompts.com. The user clicking through from a "ChatGPT prompt for cover letter" page is not in the market for Aaron Sorkin's screenwriting class.
- Network is ShareASale (not CJ, despite what most affiliate-review blogs claim — verify on application). Different publisher account from Impact, so one more thing to maintain.

**When to revisit:** if you ever build a creative-writing or lifestyle vertical that overlaps MasterClass's catalog (cooking, music, photography). Not aligned with the current career/business/productivity skew.

## Implementation notes

- **One affiliate per prompt page slot, not multiple.** The current "Go Deeper" structure has one Books block (Amazon). Add one Courses block under it, with 2–3 hand-picked Skillshare links per page, then Coursera once approved. Don't put both networks on the same page — clutters the section and makes it look like an affiliate farm.
- **Disclosure:** the existing site already has `/affiliate-disclosure.html` linked in the footer and a one-liner at the bottom of every prompt page's "Go Deeper" block ("Some of these links are affiliate links..."). That stays. No new disclosure needed; the same one covers Skillshare and Coursera under FTC rules.
- **Tagging convention:** use a UTM-style path append (`?utm_source=snipprompts&utm_medium=goingdeeper&utm_campaign={slug}`) so you can see which prompt pages drive which conversions inside Impact's report view.
- **Link freshness:** course URLs rot. Build a yearly "audit affiliate links" task into the calendar. Skillshare's URLs are stable; Coursera's individual-course URLs drift, but the Specialization URLs are stable.
