## LONG DESCRIPTION (paste into the main editor)

## The problem the bundle solves

Every ChatGPT job-search prompt on the open internet has the same failure mode: it lets the model invent. Numbers you never had. Roles you didn't hold. "Spearheaded cross-functional synergy" for jobs that didn't have synergy. Cover letters that begin with "I am writing to express my strong interest" — the exact line every recruiter has read ten thousand times.

That's why your applications go quiet. Not because your background is weak; because the AI is helpfully filling gaps with stuff that falls apart in the first follow-up question.

## What this bundle is

44 prompts + 8 negotiation scripts + 3 worksheets, organized as a single workflow — resume → cover letter → LinkedIn → interview → salary negotiation. Not a folder of one-liners; an opinionated walkthrough of the whole job search.

Every prompt is built around three guardrails:

- **Refuse-to-invent gates.** The model is explicitly instructed to ASK rather than fabricate when input is thin. The bundle's prompts are 5–15× longer than the free versions because the gates take real machinery.
- **Banned-phrase lists.** Each prompt carries the corpus-specific list of clichés it must not emit. "Results-driven," "proven track record," "passionate about" — every cliché recruiters skim past is named and blocked at the prompt level.
- **Post-output validation.** Every prompt ends with the model re-reading its own output against the rules above and flagging slips. You catch what it misses; it catches what you miss.

## What's in it

- **Module 1 — Resume:** 8 prompts including the verification-table version (every output bullet traces back to a line in your input). Plus an ATS-readability checklist and 3 before/after rewrites.
- **Module 2 — Cover Letter:** 6 prompts including the "specific opener" generator, the skim-for-cheat-sheet, and 3 opener templates per role type.
- **Module 3 — LinkedIn:** 8 prompts. Headline generator, profile-section rewriter, 14-item optimization checklist, 10-post-type weekly content pack.
- **Module 4 — Interview:** 12 prompts. Story-mining workflow, 3-round AI-grilling drill, answer-scoring prompt, behavioral and technical variants.
- **Module 5 — Salary Negotiation (flagship):** 10 prompts + 8 copy-paste scripts. BATNA worksheet, comp-research template, walkaway-math calculator. Scripts cover base salary, equity, signing bonus, relocation, remote work, title bump, start date, and competing-offer leverage.

Total: 118 pages, single PDF + a Notion workspace if you prefer that format.
