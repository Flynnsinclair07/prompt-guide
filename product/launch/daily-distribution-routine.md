# Daily Distribution Routine — SnipPrompts post-launch

**Purpose:** the 60-min/day framework that compounds for 90 days. Without this, "I'll build more pages" wins and traffic stays flat.

**Created:** 2026-06-02, after the PH launch flopped + the IH post-mortem went live + batches 1-3 of career-center outreach (54 emails) shipped.

**Realistic time to first signal:** 60-90 days. If you maintain this for 3 months, the first real "huh, something's working" data will show. Quitting at month 2 is the trap.

---

## The 60 minutes (5 weekdays)

### 15 min — Inbox triage

**When:** first thing in the morning, before checking GA4 or anything else.

- Open `flynnsinclair07@icloud.com`
- Reply to any career-center director who replied — same day, short (1-3 sentences), no sales-y push
- For auto-replies, log in `career-centers-tracker.md`, no reply needed
- For bounces, retry to the fallback address using `career-centers-bounce-fallbacks.md`
- Note any user feedback or DMs from the IH post, snipprompts.com contact, etc.

### 20 min — Community presence (3-4 days/week)

**Pick ONE per session. Same community, same username, slow burn.**

Option A: **r/cscareerquestions** — 2 substantive comments
- Sort by `New`. Find threads where someone's asking a specific question about resumes, cover letters, interview prep, LinkedIn, or salary negotiation
- Reply with genuinely useful advice. Drop a snipprompts URL ONLY if a specific prompt page directly answers the question
- Don't link in every comment. Most should be link-free pure help. Build karma + recognition first.

Option B: **Indie Hackers** — 2 substantive comments on OTHER posts (not yours)
- Browse Growth / Milestones / Show IH groups
- Reply where you have a real perspective (your launch data, what worked/didn't, distribution lessons)
- Don't promote. Build profile recognition for when your future posts ship.

Option C: **Hacker News** — 1 thoughtful comment on a relevant top-50 thread
- Threads about AI for resumes, ChatGPT prompts, job hunting in 2026, the AI flood problem
- Real value-add comment with one specific insight. Drop snipprompts URL only if directly relevant.

Option D: **Discord/Slack** (if you join any AI/founder communities)
- One substantive contribution per session. Same rules.

### 20 min — Original content (2-3 days/week)

**Pick ONE per session.**

Option A: **IH post** when you have real new data
- After a notable bounce, reply, or first sale
- After a Pinterest pin hits
- After Show HN data is in (Wed or Thu)
- Real story, real numbers, real lessons

Option B: **Newsletter to Kit list** — once you have 50+ subscribers
- One new prompt + one short reflection
- Send Wed or Thu morning

Option C: **LinkedIn post** (personal, if you decide to use it)
- One short story, one specific lesson, one concrete number
- Not a launch announcement. A "what I learned this week" post.

Option D: **X/Twitter thread** — when you finally have the account
- Use the 10 pre-drafted threads in `twitter-threads-10-launch-week.md`
- One per week max

### 5 min — Buffer

**Every day. Quick admin so the system doesn't drift.**

- Schedule next Pinterest pin from the queue (60 pins ready; ~1 every 2 days)
- Add notes to relevant tracker files as you go
- Note one thing you learned today in a running "lessons" doc

---

## The 2 days off

**Saturday + Sunday:** rest. No site work. No inbox. No checking GA4. The work compounds without you for 48 hours; the recovery compounds your next 5 days.

**Exception:** if a career-center director or PH/HN comment lands on Sat/Sun that needs a same-day reply, take 5 min. Otherwise off.

---

## What NOT to do during the daily hour

These feel productive and produce ~zero impact:

- **Building more pages.** You have 230. Stop.
- **Tweaking existing copy.** Unless a specific signal tells you a page is broken, leave it.
- **Redesigning the homepage.** No traffic to optimize for yet.
- **Polishing OG images.** Marginal at best.
- **Researching new outreach lists for 2 hours.** Spend max 15 min on research per session.
- **Checking GA4 daily.** Weekly is enough. Daily will make you crazy.
- **Replying to scammy/spammy cold emails** (Talon, ReleaseLog domain pitches, Beryxa rebrand, etc.). Delete.

## Weekly review (Friday afternoon, 15 min)

- Total visitors this week (from GA4)
- New email subscribers this week (from Kit)
- New career-center replies this week (from inbox)
- IH/Reddit comments/upvotes this week (mental count)
- One thing that surprised me this week
- One thing I'll do differently next week

Add to `product/launch/post-launch-week-X-plan.md` (rotate weekly).

## Realistic expectations by month

- **Month 1 (June):** 10-50 visitors/day. 0-2 sales. 1-3 career-center replies trickle in. First IH thread fades, second one possibly lands. Show HN one-shot result known by end of week 1.
- **Month 2 (July):** 50-150 visitors/day. 1-5 sales. Pinterest pins start producing traffic. SEO long-tail starts ranking on obscure terms. Maybe 1 career-center link goes live.
- **Month 3 (August):** 150-400 visitors/day. 3-10 sales. First clear "this channel is working" data. Maybe time to set up X/Twitter for real and start the thread queue.

If you're below month 1 numbers by mid-July, that's actually still on track — distribution lags. Don't panic and start rebuilding.

If you're above month 3 numbers in June, that's not normal — verify the GA4 events aren't double-counting and that the visitors aren't bots.

---

## When to deviate

**Deviate UP (do more):** if you get a real lead (a podcaster who wants to interview you, a career center director who wants to do something formal, a press inquiry). Then drop the routine, respond fast, take the win.

**Deviate DOWN (do less):** if you genuinely don't have 60 min one day, do the 15-min inbox triage and skip the rest. One missed day doesn't break the system. Three missed days in a row means something's off — go to bed earlier.

**Don't deviate sideways:** if you "feel like" building a new page, that's the trap. Save the urge in a doc called `things-i-want-to-build-when-traffic-justifies-it.md` and do the routine instead.
