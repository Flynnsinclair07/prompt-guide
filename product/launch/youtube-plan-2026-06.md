# YouTube channel plan — SnipPrompts

**Format split:** voice-only screen recording (long form, 8-12 min) + faceless slideshow (short form, 3-5 min). No on-camera face. Your voice + your screen + your text overlays.

**Cadence:** 1 video per week minimum. Same day every week. Default: Sundays 7 PM MT for upload (gets a US Sunday-evening + Monday-morning lift on the algorithm).

**Realistic expectations:** YouTube is the slowest-compounding channel of all the ones you're running. First 10 videos build the channel; first 30-50 videos start to compound. 6-12 months minimum before meaningful subscribers. Plan to commit to 90 days (12 videos) before judging whether it's working.

## Equipment (use what you have)

**Microphone:** Mac built-in is fine for first 5 videos. If you keep going past month 1, $80 Samson Q2U USB mic is the upgrade. Don't buy gear before you've shipped 5 videos.

**Screen recording:** Mac built-in.
- Press **Cmd+Shift+5** → pick "Record Selected Portion" or "Record Entire Screen"
- Click the **"Options"** dropdown → set Microphone to "MacBook Pro Microphone" (or whichever Mac mic you have)
- Hit Record
- After: spacebar trims, export, done

**Editing:** iMovie (free, built-in) is enough. Don't buy Final Cut Pro yet.

**Slideshow tool (for the slideshow format):** Keynote (free, built-in) → export to video. Or Canva → record presentation → export.

**Total cost to start:** $0.

## Voice-only screen recording — what each video looks like

Length: 8-12 min.

Structure:
1. **Hook (0:00-0:15)** — name the specific failure. Example: "ChatGPT invented a 40% revenue increase I never made. Here's why, and how to stop it."
2. **What you're going to show (0:15-0:45)** — preview the prompt + the result
3. **Walk through the prompt page** (0:45-3:00) — share screen, scroll the page, explain what each section does
4. **Live demo** (3:00-7:00) — open ChatGPT, paste the prompt, fill in real-ish inputs, narrate as the model responds
5. **Critique the output** (7:00-9:00) — what worked, what's still weak, what you'd refine
6. **Where to find the prompt** (9:00-9:30) — direct URL on snipprompts.com, free, no signup
7. **CTA + sign-off** (9:30-10:00) — subscribe if you want more of these, link in description

Brand voice: lowercase, direct, no exclamation points, no "hey what's up YouTube fam," no music sting. Match your IH/site voice.

## Faceless slideshow — what each video looks like

Length: 3-5 min.

Structure:
1. **Hook slide (0:00-0:08)** — one bold headline. Example: "4 things recruiters cut in 8 seconds."
2. **Why this matters (0:08-0:30)** — one or two sentences of context. Slide with one stat.
3. **The list (0:30-3:30)** — one slide per item, narrated. Each item: the problem, the fix, one example.
4. **Where to learn more (3:30-4:00)** — slide with URL. Mention the prompt page.
5. **Sign-off (4:00-4:30)** — short and clean.

Keynote/Canva slide template:
- NAVY background (#1a1a2e)
- CREAM headline (#F5EBDD)
- Big font (60-100pt for headlines)
- One idea per slide, minimal text

## Don't deviate

- **Don't record before your voice is fully back.** A scratchy voice in video 1 sets the tone of the channel for everyone who finds it later. Wait 3-7 more days.
- **Don't spend $200 on a mic before video 5.** Mac built-in is fine.
- **Don't make a logo intro or outro animation.** Adds nothing, costs minutes per video.
- **Don't use music.** Voice-only is the brand. Music would feel manufactured.
- **Don't post short-form clips to Shorts as a separate strategy yet.** Maybe month 4. Focus on long-form first.
- **Don't read the comments daily for the first month.** You'll get 0-3 per video. Check weekly.
