# Career Centers — Batch 2 Personalization Hooks

Research date: 2026-05-21. Hooks verified against career-center pages and recent news.

---

### James Madison University (JMU)
- **Hook (1 sentence):** The JMU Career Closet (run jointly with The Pantry) lets students take up to five pieces of professional attire free each semester — a concrete commitment to lowering the cost of "looking the part" for interviews.
- **Source URL:** https://www.jmu.edu/career/events/career-closet.shtml
- **Bridge to snipprompts.com:** Just as the Career Closet removes the cost barrier to professional attire, snipprompts removes the cost/skill barrier to professional written materials (resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn) — same philosophy, different bottleneck.

---

### Butler University
- **Hook (1 sentence):** CaPS rolled out Hiration (AI resume/cover-letter/mock-interview feedback) and the Indeed Job Search Academy in fall 2025 to give students 24/7 career support — Gary's own quote was that CaPS "isn't there at 11pm when a student is at home working on their resume."
- **Source URL:** https://thebutlercollegian.com/2025/10/caps-expands-career-resources-with-24-7-support/
- **Bridge to snipprompts.com:** Butler has clearly committed to the "AI as 24/7 career coach" model — snipprompts is a complementary free library of 134 vetted ChatGPT prompts that solves the "I have ChatGPT open but don't know what to ask it" problem that AI tools like Hiration don't address.

> Note: This is the strongest hook of the five — Beaulieu has publicly invested in exactly the category snipprompts serves.

---

### University of Dayton
- **Hook (1 sentence):** Eckert's team just reported a 97% first-destination success rate for the August 2024 – May 2025 class (avg starting salary $61,279) and runs an explicit "using AI in the job hunt" workshop series each semester through Handshake.
- **Source URL:** https://udayton.edu/news/articles/2026/04/flyer-first-destination-survey.php
- **Bridge to snipprompts.com:** UD already trains students on AI for the job hunt — snipprompts can serve as the take-home prompt library students reference after the workshop ends, extending the workshop's shelf life.

---

### University of Puget Sound
- **Hook (1 sentence):** Lisa's team runs Logger Link — Puget Sound's PeopleGrove-powered alumni mentorship network — with a formal matched program that ran Oct 2024 – April 2025 connecting students to alumni mentors in their field.
- **Source URL:** https://www.pugetsound.edu/alumni/alumni-community/logger-link-puget-sounds-mentorship-network
- **Bridge to snipprompts.com:** Logger Link helps students with the "who do I talk to" side of careers; snipprompts helps with the "what do I say / what do I write" side — specifically the LinkedIn outreach, informational-interview prep, and follow-up email prompts that make mentor connections actually convert.

---

### Drake University
- **Hook (1 sentence):** Drake's "True Blue Skills" framework is built on employer survey research and frames career prep as helping students "build a story worth telling" through behaviors and soft skills rather than just credentials.
- **Source URL:** https://www.drake.edu/career/trueblueskills/
- **Bridge to snipprompts.com:** "A story worth telling" is exactly what snipprompts' resume and cover-letter prompts are designed to surface — students who can identify a True Blue Skill but can't articulate it in writing are the exact use case.
