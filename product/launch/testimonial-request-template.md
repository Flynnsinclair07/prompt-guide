# Testimonial Request — first buyers

Goal: turn the first handful of Job Hunter's AI Bundle buyers into a few honest, specific testimonials we can put on the Gumroad listing, the homepage, and the PH page. Specific beats glowing. One real line about what changed ("rewrote my resume in an afternoon and got two callbacks") is worth more than five-star adjectives.

## When to ask
- **Gumroad auto-review:** Gumroad already emails buyers asking for a rating after purchase. Leave that on — it's the lowest-friction source.
- **Direct ask:** send the email below **5–7 days after purchase** — long enough they've used it, soon enough they remember. Send from `flynnsinclair07@icloud.com`.
- Don't ask the same person twice. If they don't reply, let it go.

## What to ask for (make it easy)
Give them 2–3 specific prompts so they're not staring at a blank reply:
1. What were you stuck on before you bought it?
2. What did you actually do with it?
3. What happened next (callback, offer, just "less dread")?

## Email 1 — the ask (warm, low-pressure)
> Subject: quick question about the bundle
>
> hey [name],
>
> thanks for picking up the Job Hunter's AI Bundle — I build this stuff solo, so it genuinely means a lot.
>
> if you've had a chance to use it, I'd love one or two honest sentences about how it went. no need to be polished — what were you stuck on, what did you use, and did anything come of it? even "it's fine, here's what I'd change" is useful.
>
> if it's okay to quote you (first name + role, or anonymous — your call), just say so in your reply.
>
> either way, thanks for the support.
> flynn

## Email 2 — only if they replied positively but didn't give a usable quote
> hey [name], glad it helped. mind if I use that line — "[paste their words]" — on the site with your first name and role? happy to keep it anonymous if you'd rather.

## Where testimonials go (in priority order)
1. Gumroad listing — top of the description, above the fold.
2. Homepage — a short "what buyers said" strip near the bundle banner.
3. PH page + future emails.

## Rules
- Never write the testimonial for them or edit meaning — light trims only (cut filler, keep their words).
- Always get explicit permission before quoting, and honor anonymous requests.
- Keep a running list of permitted quotes in this folder as they come in.
- No incentives for reviews (Gumroad/PH rules + it taints the signal). A genuine thank-you is enough.
