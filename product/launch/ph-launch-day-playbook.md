# Product Hunt Launch Day Playbook — Wed May 27, 2026

**Goal:** rank in the top 10 of PH for the day. The win is the backlink + traffic spike + feedback, not the rank itself.

**Times:** all MDT (Mountain Daylight Time, your timezone). PT = MDT - 1 hour. ET = MDT + 2 hours.

## Wednesday May 27 — Launch day

### 1:01 AM MDT — Launch goes live (optional to be awake)

PH auto-publishes your post. Your maker's first comment auto-posts. **No action needed if you're asleep.**

If you happen to be awake:
- Open https://www.producthunt.com — your product should be on the homepage feed
- Take a screenshot of the post going live (for memory + social proof later)
- Reply to any comments that come in within the first 30 min — early engagement signals to the algorithm
- Don't share the link anywhere yet (too early for friends to see traction)

### 7:00 AM MDT — Morning check (15-20 min)

1. **Check PH stats** (3 min)
   - Open your live post
   - Note: upvote count, comment count
   - Screenshot for the day's record

2. **Reply to every comment** (5-10 min)
   - Open each comment, hit Reply
   - Match tone of the comment (curious → curious reply; skeptical → genuine response; just an upvote with no comment → no need to reply)
   - **Never ask for upvotes** in replies (PH rule, will get flagged)
   - **Acknowledge specific points the commenter raised** — generic "thanks!" looks like a bot

3. **Pre-staged answers for the 3 most likely first comments** (already drafted in show-hn-post.md notes — adapt for PH):
   - "How is this different from awesome-chatgpt-prompts?" → "Most prompt repos are one-line 'act as a X.' Each SnipPrompts page is structured — input slots, guardrails, output validator. Closer to a function signature than a recipe."
   - "Can you show me a strong example prompt?" → Link to interview-prep or negotiation page directly (your higher-craft ones)
   - "What about the $39 bundle?" → "Bundle is the deep version — 44 prompts, full job-search arc, refuse-to-invent gates. Free pages are the front door. Either way works."

4. **Check email** (2 min) — any career center director replies? Same-day response is high-leverage.

### 8:00 AM MDT — Coffee + morning share (15 min)

1. **Share the PH link in 3 places** (don't spam):
   - **Indie Hackers** — if your milestone post unlocked by now, post it. Otherwise comment on someone else's PH-launch post saying "we're also launching today — link if curious"
   - **Twitter (skip — no personal social per the strategy)**
   - **Personal Slack/Discord groups** — only the ones where it's not spammy ("hey we're live on PH today, would love feedback if you're around")

2. **DO NOT post in r/ProductHunt or PH-upvote groups** — instant ban + PH delisting.

3. **Email Kit subscribers** — Email 5 should auto-send via Kit broadcast (see Email 5 doc). If not, manually trigger it.

### 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM MDT — Reply window (check every 30 min)

This is the heaviest comment hour. New PH users browse during US morning commute (ET + PT).

**Cadence:**
- Every 30 min, refresh your PH post
- Reply to any new comment within 30 min of it being posted
- Drink water. Don't doomscroll the upvote count — replying is what matters, not refreshing

**Be aware of:**
- **The skeptic comment** — "yet another prompt collection" or "AI doesn't replace careful writing" — engage genuinely, don't argue, acknowledge their concern, offer a specific example
- **The pitch comment** — someone trying to pivot to plug their own product — politely thank, no link back
- **The feedback comment** — "the resume one is too generic" — say "fair, what would make it stronger?" These are the highest-leverage replies — turn critics into commenters

### 12:00 PM MDT — Lunch (30 min, no PH)

**Actually step away.** Eat. Don't refresh. The post will keep getting upvotes whether you're watching or not.

### 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM MDT — Afternoon (check every 30-60 min)

Lighter comment volume. Use the time to:
- Reply to email inbox (career center responses, Kit replies, Gumroad buyer questions)
- Update the career-centers-tracker.md with any responses received
- Quick check on Gumroad analytics — any sales from PH traffic?

### 5:00 PM MDT — End-of-day stats (10 min)

1. **Final upvote count screenshot**
2. **Top 3 comments to follow up on tomorrow**
3. **Gumroad sales count** — what did the launch convert?
4. **Pinterest analytics** — did PH drive a referral spike?

### 6:00 PM MDT — Closing comment (5 min)

Post one final comment on your PH post:

> "Thanks everyone for the upvotes and feedback today. Reading every comment. If you have questions about a specific prompt or want to suggest one I haven't built, drop it here — I'm reading until tonight."

This stays at the top of the comment section for stragglers in evening hours.

### 9:00 PM MDT — Wind down

- One final reply pass on any late comments
- Sleep early. Tomorrow is the post-launch crash + cleanup.

## What NOT to do on launch day

- ❌ **Don't ask for upvotes** anywhere — instant ban risk
- ❌ **Don't use upvote services** — PH detects them, you get delisted
- ❌ **Don't post in /r/ProductHunt** — same risk
- ❌ **Don't argue with critics** — engage, acknowledge, offer a specific example. Then move on
- ❌ **Don't refresh the upvote count every 5 min** — it's a black hole of dopamine. Set 30-min intervals
- ❌ **Don't get emotional about the rank** — first launches are modest. The backlink + traffic + feedback is the actual win
- ❌ **Don't drink alcohol during the day** — you need crisp replies all day. Save it for celebratory beer at 9 PM

## The realistic outcome

First-time PH launch with no email list and no audience usually gets:
- 30-80 upvotes (top 30-50 for the day)
- 5-15 comments
- 50-200 visitors from PH directly
- 0-3 sales day-of (more drip in over the next week)
- 1-2 backlinks from "today on PH" roundup sites

If you beat any of those, that's a strong day. If you fall short, that's also normal — most first launches are modest. The compound work is what matters.
