# Email 5 — Product Hunt Launch Day Broadcast

**Send via Kit broadcast on Wednesday, May 27, 2026.**

**Recommended send time:** **7:00 AM MDT** (Wednesday). That's 9 AM ET / 6 AM PT — peak morning inbox time across all US time zones. Subscribers see it with coffee.

**List size:** 4 subscribers (real number — set expectations).

**Subject options** (pick one — sentence case, lowercase per brand voice):

1. **`we're live on Product Hunt today`** — clearest, most direct. Recommended.
2. `snipprompts is on Product Hunt today` — slightly more formal.
3. `quick favor — PH launch today` — softer, more personal.

Default to #1.

**Preview text** (the line after subject in the inbox):
```
quick note from Flynn — we'd love your eyes on the PH page if you have a sec
```

## Notes

- No exclamation points (matches brand voice)
- No banned phrases
- Numbered actions for readability — subscribers can skim and pick
- "I'll be reading every comment all day" sets honest expectation (it's a small launch, you'll actually be there)
- Sign-off matches the canonical pattern (`— Flynn` / `snipprompts.com` on its own line)
- **The PH URL above is a placeholder** — replace with the real `https://www.producthunt.com/products/snipprompts` URL OR (more reliable) the post URL once it goes live at 12:01 AM PT. If you're scheduling this on Tuesday night, the live post URL won't exist yet, so use the Coming Soon URL (your followers see the same destination after launch).

## How to schedule in Kit

1. Log into https://app.kit.com (or convertkit.com if that still redirects)
2. Click **Broadcasts** in the top nav
3. Click **New Broadcast**
4. Subject line: paste from above
5. Preview text: paste from above
6. Body: paste the email body — use Kit's "Plain text" mode (not "Beautiful" / branded template). The list responded to plain-text on the May 17 launch; keep the format consistent.
7. **Recipients:** Select your full list (all 4 subscribers). No segmentation needed.
8. **Send time:** click "Schedule for later" → set to **Wednesday, May 27, 2026 at 7:00 AM MDT**
9. Confirm timezone — Kit defaults to your account timezone; check it shows MT or America/Denver
10. Click **Schedule**

**One pre-send check:** the night before (Tuesday May 26), open the scheduled broadcast and verify:
- Recipient count = 4 (or whatever your list size is on Tuesday)
- Send time still Wed May 27 7:00 AM MDT
- PH URL is up-to-date (if PH has changed their slug, fix)

## Don't do

- **Don't add a "P.S." with an upsell.** This is a tiny list of early supporters. Treating it as a sales channel right after they signed up free-prompt is a churn risk.
- **Don't ask them to "share widely" or "tell everyone."** Specific actions (upvote, comment) convert better than vague ones.
- **Don't include the Gumroad bundle link in this email.** PH audience reaction matters today; the bundle CTA lives in the PH page's own description.
- **Don't capitalize "I" or start sentences with a capital** — matches the brand voice and the previous launch email.
- **Don't use any exclamation points.** Tempting on a launch day, banned per voice rules.
