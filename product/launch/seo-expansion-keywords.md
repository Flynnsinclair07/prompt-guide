# SEO Expansion Keywords — 40 New Prompt-Page Topics

**Summary:** This pipeline contains **18 HIGH-opportunity** topics, **17 MED**, and **5 LOW**, all cross-checked against the existing 134 pages to guarantee no duplicates. The biggest whitespace is in **Career** (the highest-converting category, feeding the Job Hunter's Bundle — and the site only has ~10 career pages despite it being the core revenue driver) and **Finance/Legal "money & high-stakes" topics**, where ChatGPT's default output is generic and risky, giving every page a clean "ChatGPT fails at this by default" angle. **Top 5 to build first:** thank-you email after interview, follow-up email after no response, salary negotiation email, two weeks notice / 30-day notice letter, and reference list / brag sheet — all career, all high-intent, all funnel directly to the bundle.

## Build-First 10 (highest opportunity, in build order)

1. **thank-you-email-after-interview** — Highest-intent moment in the funnel: someone *just* interviewed and is about to act. Bundle-adjacent, huge volume, the existing generic thank-you page doesn't capture it.
2. **follow-up-email-after-no-response** — Universal recurring pain; ChatGPT's default needy tone is a textbook "AI fails this" angle, and it pulls job seekers straight toward the bundle.
3. **salary-negotiation-email** — Money + career + high-stakes in one query; the existing negotiation page is verbal/general, leaving the lucrative "in writing" version wide open.
4. **two-weeks-notice-letter** — Distinct high-volume long-tail from the resignation page; someone quitting needs the doc *now*, and "30-day notice letter" piggybacks on the same page.
5. **reference-list / brag-sheet** — Almost no quality competition, pure bundle funnel, and a clean upsell into the full job-hunt product.
6. **recommendation-letter** — Very high volume, low competition, and ChatGPT's hollow generic praise is one of the clearest failure angles on the whole list.
7. **dispute-letter** — Money + quasi-legal; people are stressed and motivated, competition is weak, and ChatGPT's vague default makes the "you can't trust the default" pitch obvious.
8. **college-essay** — Enormous seasonal search (Aug–Nov), distinct from existing essay pages, and high emotional stakes for families.
9. **wedding-vows** — High-stakes one-shot moment, ChatGPT defaults to cliché, and the existing wedding-speech page doesn't capture the vows searcher.
10. **elevator-pitch** — High volume, bundle-adjacent, evergreen, and ChatGPT's buzzword-soup default gives an easy differentiation hook.
