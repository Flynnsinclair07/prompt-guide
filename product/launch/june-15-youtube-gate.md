# June 15 YouTube gate — go / hold decision

Run this Sunday June 15. The point of this gate is to decide whether to start a YouTube channel (a big, slow-payoff content channel) based on whether the smaller channels are working. **No YouTube before this gate, no matter what.**

## Why this gate exists
YouTube takes 50–100+ hours before it returns measurable traffic. If the existing channels (SEO, Pinterest, career outreach, PH, Show HN, IH, Reddit) are working, YouTube compounds; if they aren't, YouTube doesn't fix the problem — it just buries you in production work.

| Signal | Threshold | Your value |

### What to do — by outcome

**GREEN (3 of 4 met):** Start YouTube. The existing channels prove the product converts; YouTube layers on top. First video: a 5-minute walkthrough of the 4-failure-modes framework from the pillar article. Script lives in the long-form guides on the site.

**YELLOW (2 of 4):** HOLD YouTube. Do another 3 weeks of compounding the channels that ARE working. Re-run this gate on July 6. The lowest-effort thing that moves a YELLOW number to GREEN is usually: publish 5–10 more SEO pages from the pipeline (`seo-expansion-keywords.md`), each one a near-zero-marginal-cost lever after the first batch.

**RED (0–1 of 4):** Don't start YouTube. Don't even open Premiere. The harder question is: are the channels themselves broken (audience fit, message, product-market) or just early? Answer that question first. Email me and we'll diagnose.

## Anti-rules
- Do NOT use feelings as inputs. Use the numbers in the table.
- Do NOT compare to a creator who's been on YouTube for 2 years. They have audience momentum you don't.
- Do NOT start "just one video to see." Either you commit to a 30-video minimum or you don't start. YouTube doesn't reward half-effort.
- Do NOT pivot the bundle based on this gate. The gate is about CHANNEL strategy, not PRODUCT strategy.
