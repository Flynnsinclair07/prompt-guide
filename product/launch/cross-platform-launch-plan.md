# Cross-platform launch plan — what to ship next, in what order

PH launches May 27. That's the headline. But PH is one event in a longer distribution sequence — and the products that hit the trending charts on five launch platforms get a multiplier effect because indie hackers, journalists, and acquirers cross-reference.

This is the order I'd ship in.

---

## Tier 1 — already locked or imminent

### Product Hunt — May 27, 2026 (this Wednesday)
- **Status:** scheduled, auto-publishes 12:01 AM PT.
- **What it gets you:** ~1,000-5,000 visitors on a successful day, modest direct sales (PH crowd is mostly other founders), and a permanent backlink from one of the highest-DR sites in the indie space.
- **Your job:** reply to every comment on phone breaks Wednesday. Don't ask for upvotes. Don't ghost the thread on Thursday.

### Show HN — Tuesday June 2, 8-10 AM Eastern
- **Status:** copy ready in `show-hn-post.md`, your hands.
- **What it gets you:** the HN crowd is older, more technical, and skeptical. A successful Show HN brings 2,000-8,000 visitors and the most thoughtful feedback you'll ever get on a product. A failed Show HN gets ignored. Both are useful.
- **Pre-flight:** title is under 80 chars. Body is 150-250 words. First comment in the thread is from you and engages with the first substantive critique.

## Tier 2 — launch in week 2-3 (June 2-13)

### Tiny Launch (tinylaunch.com) — June 4
- **Why:** small but real audience of solo builders. Great for finding your first 10 paying buyers who'll give you real feedback.
- **Format:** under-100-word post + one screenshot. Almost no submission friction.
- **Ask:** "what would you have done differently if you launched a $39 prompt bundle?" — the audience pays attention to that kind of question.

### Hacker News Show HN follow-up — June 9 (if first Show HN dies)
- **Why:** if the Show HN on June 2 didn't trend, a re-submission a week later with a different title can land. The crowd resets.
- **Different angle:** lead with the long-form article ("how I built a 167-prompt library by following one rule") rather than the bundle itself.

### Indie Hackers milestone post — when you hit the first 5 sales
- **Why:** IH's "Milestones" section rewards specifics. "First $X" with a real story is the format that hits home.
- **Status:** copy outlined in `indie-hackers-post.md`. Your account unlocks for posting when karma builds — drop 1 genuine comment per day until unlocked.

## Tier 3 — launch in week 3-5 (June 14 - July 4)

### Uneed (uneed.best) — June 14-18
- **Why:** AI-tools directory with a small but genuinely engaged audience. Pricing has a free tier; paid placement is reasonable.
- **Format:** product card with link, pricing, screenshot. Low effort.
- **Honest read:** Uneed's traffic is small but the multiplier comes from being cross-referenced in "best AI tools for X" listicles — those listicles scrape Uneed.

### BetaList (betalist.com) — June 18-22
- **Why:** good for early-adopter buzz; the audience skews tech and product-curious.
- **Best for:** the pre-launch list (mailing list growth), not direct conversions. Your hook here is "join the waitlist for [next version of bundle]" rather than "buy now."

### Reddit r/SaaS, r/Entrepreneur, r/sideproject — June 16-30
- **Status:** archetype replies drafted in `reddit-prep.md`. Account `Ok_Penalty1888` is warming.
- **Approach:** ONE post per subreddit, 7+ days apart. Lead with the lesson, not the product. Mention the URL in the comments only after questions appear.

### "Best of" listicles outreach — last week of June
- **Why:** outlets like Failory, Indie Hackers Blog, Starter Story, and AI-tools listicles regularly publish "best prompt libraries for X" pieces. A short, useful pitch with the press kit gets you mentioned.
- **Format:** see `/press.html`. Email subject line: "Press kit: SnipPrompts (164+ ChatGPT prompts, launched on PH May 27)."
- **Target:** 10 outlets in 1 hour of outreach.

## Tier 4 — long tail (July+)

### Subreddit owned content — month 2-3
- Build genuine presence in 2 subreddits where your ICP hangs out. Aim for one community where you're "the AI prompts person" before any product mention.
- This is months of work for one outcome (compounding referral traffic) but the highest-quality buyers come from these threads.

### Podcasts — month 2+
- IndieHackers Podcast (Courtland Allen), My First Million, This Week in Startups (small-business segment), various solopreneur shows.
- Pitch: see the press kit. Your hook is "19-year-old built a prompt library + bundle in 2 months pre-college."
- Don't pitch until you have one strong testimonial + the demo video shipped. Otherwise the conversation lacks artifacts to point at.

### YouTube — wait for the June 15 gate
- Don't start YouTube until the post-PH data clears the gate (5+ sales OR 1K weekly impressions sustained).
- If gate passes: long-form video on the four failure modes (a video version of the pillar article) is the highest-leverage first piece.
- If gate fails: hold. YouTube is the most time-expensive channel; do not invest until you have demand signal elsewhere.

## Cross-platform rules

1. **Same product, different framing per platform.** PH wants "look at this new thing." HN wants "here's an interesting technical idea." IH wants "here's what I learned shipping this." Tiny Launch wants "here's a small thing I built." The product is the same. The story is different.

2. **Cross-link assets, not pitches.** When you launch on HN, the body link is the homepage (not the Gumroad bundle). Once people are on the site, the funnel does its work.

3. **Calendar everything in one place.** Add each launch date to your calendar (with reminders for prep day before). Missing a launch window means the next opening is 3+ weeks away.

4. **One launch event at a time.** Two simultaneous launches dilute attention on both threads. Better to nail Show HN cleanly than to land it on the same day as Tiny Launch.

5. **The point isn't ranking on any single platform.** The point is being the product that newcomers see on PH + see again on HN two weeks later + see referenced in an IH milestone post + see in a Reddit thread. Cross-referencing compounds.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't launch on every platform in week 1.** Saturating yourself in week 1 is how you burn out and lose energy by week 3. The product will still be there.
- **Don't post the same body text on PH + HN + IH the same day.** The audiences notice and downvote.
- **Don't pay for placement on these platforms early.** Uneed, BetaList, etc. all sell premium placement. Organic placement is fine for now; pay only if you have data showing the channel converts.
- **Don't pitch journalists with "we launched on Product Hunt!"** That's not news to them. Pitch with the angle — "19-year-old built a 167-prompt library on the thesis that AI default output is bad."
- **Don't get demoralized by quiet weeks.** Most launches look like nothing for 30-90 days, then suddenly traffic shows up because three things you did months ago compounded. The math rewards consistency.
