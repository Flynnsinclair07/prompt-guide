# Teaser video — 30-sec storyboard + shot list

Companion to `teaser-video-script-30s.md`. This breaks the 30 seconds into 2-3 sec frames with exactly what's on screen, what's said, and how it's captured. Frame-by-frame so you can record without thinking.

## Setup before you hit record
- **Mic:** plug in your USB/condenser. Check input in System Settings → Sound → Input. Bar should bounce when you speak.
- **Quiet room:** close the door, kill the AC if loud, no fans.
- **Two browser tabs ready:** Tab 1 = ChatGPT (free) with a generic resume prompt typed but NOT run. Tab 2 = snipprompts.com homepage.
- **Screen recording:** `Cmd+Shift+5` → Record Selected Portion → set the rectangle to the Chrome window. Mic ON.
- **Practice the script out loud** 3 times BEFORE you hit record. Read it natural, not like a presentation.

## Editing notes

- **Cuts must be hard, no fades.** Faded transitions read as agency video; hard cuts read as builder video.
- **No music bed.** Your voice IS the value. Music turns it into an ad.
- **Color grading:** none. Raw screen capture. Slight contrast bump if anything looks washed out.
- **Captions:** burn in subtitles for the spoken parts. 70% of mobile viewers watch on mute. Use a clean white sans-serif with a black drop-shadow.
- **Aspect ratios:** export TWO versions
  - 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) → X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Instagram Reels
  - 16:9 horizontal (1920×1080) → Gumroad gallery slot
- **Length tolerance:** 28–31 seconds is fine. Under 28 = rushed; over 32 = drops on X.
- **File size:** keep both versions under 25 MB so they upload inline everywhere.

## After recording — quality check

Watch it back ONCE without sound. Does the video tell the story by itself? If yes, the captions + visuals are doing their job. If no, adjust the visuals.

Watch it again WITH sound at 1× and 1.5×. If 1.5× sounds like a normal pace, you read too fast on 1×. Re-record.

Show it to one person. If they say "this is so good" you've done something wrong (the polish is too high). If they say "yeah I'd click that" you've nailed the builder vibe.

## After publishing

- **Gumroad gallery:** add the 16:9 version to slot 2 or 3 of the listing gallery (not slot 1 — slot 1 should be the cover image).
- **Homepage:** consider embedding as an autoplay-muted hero video later, but NOT for the PH launch (autoplay can hurt LCP).
- **PH update comment:** post the video as a top-level comment on your PH listing 3 days post-launch ("update: demo's up"). PH listings keep getting traffic for 2 weeks; a day-3 update is a second engagement spike.
- **X / LinkedIn:** post the 9:16 version with a one-line caption matching the script's opening line.

## What NOT to do

- Don't shoot yourself on camera. The product IS the screen recording; your face is a distraction.
- Don't add "follow me for more" or "subscribe" CTAs. The CTA is the URL; that's it.
- Don't say "let me show you" or "watch this." Action verbs only.
- Don't re-record more than 3 times. Slight roughness reads as builder; over-polish reads as agency.
