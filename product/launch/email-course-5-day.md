## Day 3 — Cover letters: kill the two phrases that scream AI

**Subject:** the two phrases that instantly mark your cover letter as AI
**Send:** 1 day after Day 2

```
hey,

most ChatGPT cover letters open with "I am writing to express my strong interest in the [role]" and close with "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss further."

recruiters have read both ten thousand times. they mark you as "used AI, didn't edit."

the fix is two rules:
1. ban the clichés (results-driven, passionate about, thrive in, dynamic, and the two phrases above)
2. open with one specific reason you're applying — something only you know (you used their product, attended a talk, read a postmortem)

ChatGPT can write the middle once you've given it the specific hook. it can't generate the hook — that has to come from you.

## Day 4 — Interviews: stop memorizing, start drilling

**Subject:** memorizing your interview answers is making you sound worse
**Send:** 1 day after Day 3

```
hey,

the most common interview-prep mistake: paste the question into ChatGPT, get a perfect STAR answer, memorize it, deliver it word-for-word.

it backfires. written prose in your mouth sounds rehearsed, and the interviewer hears the seams by sentence two. worse, they ask a follow-up that wasn't in your script and you freeze.

the fix is to use ChatGPT differently for interviews than for written stuff:
1. have it mine your background for 5-7 stories (not write answers)
2. draft each story in bullets, not paragraphs
3. tell the story out loud to your phone — different words every time
4. have ChatGPT play the toughest interviewer and grill you on follow-ups

you bring the words. ChatGPT brings the structure and the pushback.

before any salary call, answer these four:
1. if this offer goes away today, what do I actually do?
2. what's the comp value of that path?
3. is it higher, similar, or lower than this offer?
4. am I confident I'd take it, or am I bluffing myself?

your answers decide how hard you can push. BATNA higher than the offer = walk-away leverage. BATNA lower = counter from market data, not leverage.
