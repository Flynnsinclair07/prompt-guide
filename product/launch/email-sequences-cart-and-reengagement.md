## Kit re-engagement sequence (3 emails)

**Triggers**: subscriber hasn't opened a Kit broadcast in 60 days. Goal: re-engage if possible, clean the list if not. Removes dead subscribers so open rates stay accurate.

**Setup steps (numbered, with URLs):**
1. Log in to https://app.kit.com
2. Click **Automations** → **Sequences** → **+ Create new**
3. Name it: `Re-engagement 60-day`
4. Add the 3 emails below in order, with delays: Email 1 immediate, Email 2 +3 days, Email 3 +7 days.
5. After Email 3, add an automation: tag as "inactive" and remove from main broadcast list.
6. Set the entry rule to a segment: subscribers with **last opened > 60 days ago** AND **subscribed > 60 days ago** (excludes new subscribers who haven't had time to engage yet).
7. Save and turn on.

## Notes on the sequence

**Why the 60-day window**: 30 days is too short for someone on PTO or in a slow job-search phase. 90 days is too long — by then the subscriber has forgotten the brand and the re-engagement reads as cold outreach. 60 days is the sweet spot for a weekly-prompt list.

**Why three emails, not five**: a 5-email re-engagement sequence reads as desperate to a subscriber who's already disengaged. Three is enough to give a genuine "still want this?" prompt without harassing.

**Why offer a pause option**: many people unsubscribe because they want a temporary break, not a permanent exit. A pause option captures those subscribers for later.

**Why offer the verification table as a freebie in cart Email 2**: this is the highest-value standalone artifact in the bundle. Giving it away to abandoned-cart visitors does three things: (1) builds goodwill with people who almost bought, (2) creates a list of warm leads who got a useful thing and might convert later, (3) doesn't cannibalize the bundle because the verification table alone is one piece of a 5-module set.

**Tracking**:
- Cart Email 1: target 8-12% open rate, 1-3% conversion.
- Cart Email 2: target 6-10% open rate, 0.5-1.5% conversion.
- Re-engagement 1: target 15-20% open rate (these are former-engaged subscribers).
- Re-engagement 2: target 10-15% open rate.
- Re-engagement 3: target 8-12% open rate.

If the cart sequence converts above 3% combined, leave it. If below 1.5%, rewrite Email 1's subject line first.
