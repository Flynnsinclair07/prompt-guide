## The decision matrix

| Headline metric | RED | YELLOW | GREEN |

### What to do — by bucket

**If GREEN on 2+ metrics:**
- Amplify. The Show HN post (June 2) is no longer the *next* traffic event; you have momentum NOW.
- Send a follow-up Kit broadcast Friday May 29 with "thanks for the response + here's what happened" tone (no upsell — pure community).
- Post a "we launched and here's the result" thread on LinkedIn/X linking the PH page, the homepage, AND one prompt page.
- Bring forward the demo video recording to this weekend (no longer "post-launch") if your voice is back.

**If YELLOW on most metrics (most likely):**
- This is fine. Most PH launches land here.
- Stay the course on the planned post-launch sequence: Show HN June 2, batch-2 career emails June 2, Pinterest pins June 7+.
- Reply to every PH comment for 5 more days; PH listings continue to send a long tail of traffic.
- Do NOT pivot strategy. Yellow week 1 with consistent comments + outreach often turns green by week 4.

**If RED on 3+ metrics:**
- Don't panic. Don't pivot the product.
- Honest read: write down which metric is RED and one hypothesis for why. (PH visibility was low? Bundle CTA on listing wasn't clear? Wrong day-of-week? Listing categories were off?)
- Send the rejection-stays-warm version of the email — to anyone who clicked but didn't buy.
- The Show HN post on June 2 is the rescue traffic event. Run it as planned.
- Do NOT pull the bundle. Do NOT discount. A 4-day-old product with low traffic is not a product problem.
