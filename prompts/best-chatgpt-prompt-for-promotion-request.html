ChatGPT Prompt for Asking for a Promotion

Write the written case to your manager for a promotion — specific accomplishments, tangible impact, what the next level would do for the team. No entitled tone.

Updated May 2026.

The best ChatGPT prompt for asking for a promotion writes a one-page case for your manager built around specific accomplishments and tangible impact — not tenure, not “I've been here X years.” It bans entitled language and forces you to articulate what the next level lets the team do.

The Prompt
You are a senior HR partner who has reviewed thousands of promotion cases. Help me write the written case to my manager.

MY CURRENT ROLE: [TITLE]
TARGET ROLE (the promotion): [TITLE]
MY MANAGER'S NAME + style (data-driven / narrative / hands-off): [PASTE]
TOP 3–5 SPECIFIC ACCOMPLISHMENTS in the current role (with real numbers / outcomes): [PASTE]
WHAT THE NEXT LEVEL WOULD LET ME / THE TEAM DO that I can't do now: [PASTE]
WHAT MY MANAGER HAS PUBLICLY SAID matters for promotions (1:1 notes, calibration criteria, team meeting comments): [PASTE]

HARD RULES:
1. Do not invent accomplishments, numbers, or outcomes not in my input.
2. The case is built around impact, not tenure. Banned: “I've been here X years,” “I've been waiting for,” “it's my turn.”
3. Banned phrases: passionate about, results-driven, proven track record, dynamic, level up, take ownership.
4. Length: one page max. ~400 words.
5. If my input lacks measurable outcomes, ASK before writing — don't fabricate numbers.

OUTPUT IN ORDER:
A. The promotion-request document (or email, if you specify) — opening (clear ask), three to five specific impact stories, what the next level enables, a closing line.
B. Counter-objection prep: the three most likely pushback questions from a manager + your one-line answer to each.
C. The follow-up plan if my manager doesn't respond within a week.

How to Use This Prompt

  1. Copy the prompt and fill in the bracketed sections with your real details.
  2. Be specific. Vague input produces vague output — that's the whole point of the gates.
  3. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and read the output before using it.
  4. Edit the draft in your own voice before sending or submitting. AI output is the scaffolding, not the final.

Example Output

Opening (from a real run):

Hi Maria — I'd like to formally request a promotion to Senior Engineer in this cycle. Over the last 14 months I've owned the migration off the legacy billing service (P95 latency from 740ms → 110ms post-cutover), led the on-call rotation overhaul that cut paging volume 38% quarter-over-quarter, and onboarded two engineers to L4 — both shipped owner-level features within their first 90 days. The next level lets me take on the same role for the new fraud-detection team without backfill, and free up your time on architecture review by a meaningful chunk per week. Quick details below — happy to discuss synchronously if helpful.

Tips to Get Better Results

  • Send it in writing. The written case forces the manager to commit a written answer, which they need anyway to defend the case at calibration.
  • Lead with the ask, not the build-up. “I'd like to discuss a promotion to [title]” in sentence one. The detail goes after.
  • Tie every impact story to a metric the manager already tracks. New metrics in a promotion case look fabricated even when they aren't.
  • Do not compare yourself to a specific peer. The manager either already knows or will resent the comparison.
