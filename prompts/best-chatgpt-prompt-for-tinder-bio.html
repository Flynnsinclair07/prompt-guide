ChatGPT Prompt for a Tinder Bio

A Tinder bio that gets right-swipes from people you actually want to meet — one specific weird true thing, no "love trying new things."

Updated May 2026.

The best ChatGPT prompt for a Tinder bio writes a sub-200-character bio anchored on one specific weird true thing about you — not three vague hobbies. It bans clichés like "adventure-seeker" and "looking for my person," and ends with a hook that gives matches something concrete to open with.

The Prompt
You are a dating coach who has reviewed 10,000 dating-app profiles. Your goal: make my Tinder bio specific enough that the right stranger has something to comment on, not so generic that I look like every other profile in the queue.

ABOUT ME — real bullet dump (quirks, hobbies, jobs, weird opinions, recent obsessions, ongoing projects, the thing my friends roast me about): [PASTE]
WHAT I'M LOOKING FOR (long-term / casual / "see what happens"): [PASTE]
WHAT I'M NOT LOOKING FOR (be honest — what I'd swipe left on if I saw it): [PASTE]
TONE (funny / earnest / dry / a mix): [PASTE]
ANY HARD FILTERS the bio should communicate (e.g., kids, location, religion, dietary): [PASTE]

HARD RULES:
1. Under 200 characters. Tinder users skim.
2. Open with ONE specific weird true detail. Not three. One.
3. Banned phrases (these are the dating-app median, never use): love trying new things, adventure seeker, foodie, dog person, work hard play hard, looking for my person, fluent in sarcasm, partner in crime, ask me about, you'll never guess, my dog approves, beer enthusiast.
4. End with a HOOK — a thing a match can naturally comment on or reply to. Not a billboard.
5. Do not invent details about me that aren't in my input.

OUTPUT IN ORDER:
A. Three full bio options (each under 200 chars), built from different angles of my real input.
B. Three opening lines a match could realistically send back to each — that's how you'll know if the bio actually invites conversation.
C. The single weakest line across the three options and why.

How to Use This Prompt

  1. Copy the prompt and fill in the bracketed sections with your real details.
  2. Be specific. Vague input produces vague output — that's the whole point of the gates.
  3. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and read the output before using it.
  4. Edit the draft in your own voice before sending or submitting. AI output is the scaffolding, not the final.

Example Output

Option (from a real run):

I'm the person at the dinner who slowly turns the volume down on whatever song is playing. Make of that what you will.

Networking-engineer brain in a former-band-kid body. Currently obsessed with chess at the level where I'm bad enough to be entertaining.

Match opener: "so wait — turning down the music intentionally, or hoping no one notices?"

Tips to Get Better Results

  • Specific over funny. A weirdly specific detail is more memorable than a clever line.
  • If the bio doesn't give a match something concrete to open with, it's not done.
  • Avoid the "you'll never guess" tease. Just say the thing.
  • Read each option as if you were a stranger. Would you reply? If not, rewrite.
