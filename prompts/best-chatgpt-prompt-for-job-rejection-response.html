ChatGPT Prompt for Responding to a Job Rejection

Reply to a rejection in three short sentences that keep the door open — without the defensive “Thanks anyway” energy or the doormat “Please consider me” pitch.

Updated May 2026.

The best ChatGPT prompt for responding to a job rejection generates a short, gracious reply (three sentences max) that thanks the interviewer, references one specific thing from the conversation, and signals continued interest if a future role opens — without the defensive or apologetic tone most candidates default to.

The Prompt
You are a candidate writing a graceful reply to a job rejection. Your goal is to keep the door open for a future role at this company without sounding desperate or bitter.

THE ROLE I WAS REJECTED FROM: [TITLE at COMPANY]
WHO REJECTED ME (name + role — recruiter, hiring manager, etc.): [PASTE]
ONE SPECIFIC THING from the interview process I genuinely liked or learned: [PASTE — a person, a project they described, a piece of company strategy that came up]
TONE: warm, brief, no scripted gratitude.

HARD RULES:
1. Three sentences. Maximum four. Anything longer reads as needy.
2. Do not say “Thank you for the opportunity” as the opener — every rejected candidate writes that.
3. Do not ask for feedback in this email. (Different email, different time.)
4. Do not invent enthusiasm I didn't express in the interview.
5. Banned phrases: I am writing to, thanks anyway, please keep me in mind, I wish you the best, going forward.

OUTPUT:
A. The reply email — three sentences.
B. One optional alternative version with slightly different framing.
C. A note on whether (and when) to follow up if you have not heard from this company in 6 months.

How to Use This Prompt

  1. Copy the prompt and fill in the bracketed sections with your real details.
  2. Be specific. Vague input produces vague output — that's the whole point of the gates.
  3. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and read the output before using it.
  4. Edit the draft in your own voice before sending or submitting. AI output is the scaffolding, not the final.

Example Output

Reply (from a real run):

Hi Priya — thanks for letting me know. I really liked what you described about the way the data team handles model rollback decisions; that conversation alone was worth the day. If something opens up on that team in the future, I'd love to know — happy to stay in touch either way.

Chris

Tips to Get Better Results

  • Send within 24 hours of the rejection. After that, it reads as performative.
  • Mention something specific from the interview — proves you were actually paying attention.
  • Don't ask for feedback in the same email. That's a separate (and lower-priority) conversation.
  • Resist the urge to oversell. Three sentences. The company already decided.
