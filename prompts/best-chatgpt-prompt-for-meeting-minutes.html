ChatGPT Prompt for Meeting Minutes

Turn messy raw notes into clean meeting minutes — attendees, decisions, action items with owner and deadline, next meeting. No filler, no “discussed the topic.”

Updated May 2026.

The best ChatGPT prompt for meeting minutes extracts decisions and action items (with owner and deadline) from your raw notes, structures the output as attendees → decisions → action items → next meeting, and refuses to fill space with “discussed the topic” filler.

The Prompt
You are an experienced project manager writing meeting minutes that the team will actually use. Your goal: extract decisions and action items from raw notes, not summarize chatter.

MEETING TYPE: [stand-up / planning / steering / 1:1 / etc.]
ATTENDEES: [PASTE]
RAW NOTES (bullets, chat log, voice transcript — whatever you have): [PASTE]
FORMAT I WANT: [text / markdown / table / numbered]
DISTRIBUTION: [who's reading these and what they need]

HARD RULES:
1. Output sections, in this order: Attendees · Decisions · Action items · Open questions · Next meeting.
2. Every action item has an owner AND a deadline. If the input doesn't have one, ASK before writing.
3. Banned: “discussed the topic,” “there was a discussion about,” “the team talked about.” Replace with what was decided.
4. Decisions are explicit. If something was raised but not decided, it goes under Open questions, not Decisions.
5. Length: minutes are shorter than the meeting. Cut chatter.

OUTPUT IN ORDER:
A. The minutes.
B. A list of any action items where the owner or deadline is missing from my input (so I can fill those in before sending).
C. A one-line meta-note: “tone and clarity check” — flag anything that reads ambiguous.

How to Use This Prompt

  1. Copy the prompt and fill in the bracketed sections with your real details.
  2. Be specific. Vague input produces vague output — that's the whole point of the gates.
  3. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and read the output before using it.
  4. Edit the draft in your own voice before sending or submitting. AI output is the scaffolding, not the final.

Example Output

Decisions:

  • Sprint goal for Q3-W2 is the migration cutover. All other work paused until cutover lands.
  • Rollback criteria locked: any P95 latency >900ms post-cutover triggers automatic rollback.

Action items:

  • Maria — finalize rollback script — by Wed EOD.
  • Priya — pull baseline P95 numbers for the last 30 days — by Tue EOD.
  • Owner TBD — write the customer-facing maintenance window comms — needs owner.

Tips to Get Better Results

  • Send minutes within 24 hours. After that, the action items rot.
  • Action items without owners aren't action items — they're hopes.
  • If you didn't decide something, write that. Pretending you decided is how teams revisit the same conversation in three weeks.
  • Keep raw notes for two weeks in case anyone challenges what was decided.
