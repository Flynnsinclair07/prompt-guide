ChatGPT Prompt for a Dispute Letter

Use this free prompt to write a firm, structured dispute letter for a credit report error, billing mistake, or inaccurate charge — the kind that actually gets corrected.

ChatGPT's default dispute letter is vague and toothless — "I would like to dispute this and ask you to look into it." That gets ignored. This one states the specific error, cites your right to an investigation, and sets a clear deadline.

The Prompt
You are a consumer-rights writing assistant. Write a firm, professional dispute letter.

What I'm disputing: [CREDIT REPORT ERROR / BILLING CHARGE / INACCURATE ACCOUNT — describe it]
Who I'm writing to: [CREDIT BUREAU / COMPANY / BANK name]
The specific error and why it's wrong: [EXACT DETAIL — account number, date, amount, what's inaccurate]
What I want them to do: [REMOVE / CORRECT / REFUND — be specific]
Any evidence I have: [RECEIPTS, STATEMENTS, CONFIRMATION NUMBERS — list what you'll attach]
My account/reference number: [NUMBER]

Rules:
1. State the specific error in the first two sentences. No vague "I'd like to look into a concern."
2. Be firm and factual, never emotional or threatening. Confidence, not anger.
3. Clearly state what I want them to do and a reasonable deadline (e.g., 30 days).
4. If this is a credit-report dispute, reference my right to an investigation under the Fair Credit Reporting Act.
5. Reference the evidence I'm attaching, and ask for written confirmation of the outcome.
6. Keep it under 250 words. Add a signature block and a line listing enclosures.
7. Do not invent account numbers, dates, laws, or facts I didn't provide. Add a note at the end reminding me this is not legal advice.

How to Use This Prompt

  1. Gather your specifics first — account number, dates, amounts, and what's wrong. Vague input makes a weak letter.
  2. Fill in the brackets and paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
  3. Review it for accuracy — every fact must be true and supported by your evidence.
  4. Send by certified mail with return receipt, and keep copies of everything.

Example Output

Re: Dispute of inaccurate account — Account #XXXX-1234

To whom it concerns at Experian,

I am writing to dispute a late-payment mark reported on my account #XXXX-1234 for March 2026. This mark is inaccurate: my payment was made on March 2, 2026, eleven days before the due date, as shown on the enclosed bank statement and confirmation #A8842.

Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, I am requesting that you investigate this item and remove the inaccurate late-payment mark within 30 days. Please send me written confirmation of the correction.

Enclosures: bank statement, payment confirmation.

Sincerely,
[Name]

This is not legal advice.

Tips to Get Better Results

  • One dispute per letter. Don't bundle multiple errors — separate letters are cleaner and easier to track.
  • Attach evidence, reference it by name. "As shown on the enclosed statement" is far stronger than a claim with nothing behind it.
  • Keep a paper trail. Certified mail, return receipt, and copies of everything you send.
  • Know your limits. For large sums or legal threats, talk to a consumer-protection attorney or nonprofit credit counselor — this prompt writes the letter, it doesn't replace legal advice.
