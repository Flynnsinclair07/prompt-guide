ChatGPT Prompt for a Letter of Intent

A focused letter of intent — for a job, a grad program, a real-estate deal, or a partnership. Specific reasons, clear commitment, no "I have always been interested."

Updated May 2026.

The best ChatGPT prompt for a letter of intent writes a focused, professional letter for whatever context — job, grad program, real-estate purchase, partnership. It forces specifics about WHY this opportunity and what you'd commit to, avoiding generic "I have always been interested in" openers.

The Prompt
You are a clear writer helping someone produce a letter of intent that signals real commitment without overselling. Your job is to make the reader believe this applicant has thought about it.

CONTEXT (what this letter of intent is for): [JOB OFFER / GRAD PROGRAM / REAL-ESTATE PURCHASE / BUSINESS PARTNERSHIP / OTHER — be specific]
WHO I'M WRITING TO (name + role + organization): [PASTE]
WHAT I'M COMMITTING TO (the specific thing — accepting the offer, applying to the program, buying the property, joining the partnership): [PASTE]
WHY THIS SPECIFIC OPPORTUNITY (not the field or the area generally — THIS one): [PASTE]
WHAT I WILL DO / BRING / CONTRIBUTE in concrete terms: [PASTE]
TIMELINE (when I'd start, close, commit): [PASTE]
CONDITIONS or CONTINGENCIES (anything that has to be true for this commitment): [PASTE]

HARD RULES:
1. Length: 200–400 words. A letter of intent that's too long signals uncertainty.
2. Banned phrases: ever since I was young, I have always been interested in, I am writing to express, broaden my horizons, results-driven, passionate about.
3. Open with the specific commitment in the first sentence — not a build-up.
4. Include at least one specific reason for THIS organization/program/property that wouldn't apply to others.
5. State conditions plainly if any (e.g. "contingent on inspection," "subject to financing approval," "after completing my current degree"). Don't soften them.

OUTPUT IN ORDER:
A. The letter of intent, formatted for the context (job = professional email, grad = formal letter, real estate = formal letter, etc.).
B. The specific opening sentence I should consider replacing — the version where the model would have used a cliché.
C. A one-line note on signing/submission: which format the recipient prefers (digital signature, hard-copy mail, portal upload, email) if I told you context.

How to Use This Prompt

  1. Copy the prompt and fill in the bracketed sections with your real details.
  2. Be specific. Vague input produces vague output — that's the whole point of the gates.
  3. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and read the output before using it.
  4. Edit the draft in your own voice before sending or submitting. AI output is the scaffolding, not the final.

Example Output

Opening (job offer acceptance, from a real run):

Dear Maria — I'd like to formally accept the Senior Engineer offer for the platform team, with a proposed start date of June 30, 2026. The work on the migration off the legacy billing service is exactly the engineering problem I want to spend the next two years on, and the way you described the rollback architecture in our second conversation convinced me the team thinks about reliability the way I do. I have one condition: I need to give my current employer three weeks of notice and would like to confirm June 30 in writing before I do that this week...

Tips to Get Better Results

  • Lead with the commitment, not the build-up. "I'd like to formally commit to..." beats "I am writing to express..."
  • Name the specific thing (the role title, the program name, the property address). Generic intent letters look like template forwards.
  • State conditions plainly. "Subject to inspection" is professional; vague language reads as cold feet.
  • If you're hesitating, that's signal — fix the reason before sending, not the wording.
